# Feature Specification: OmniStock MVP

**Feature Branch**: `001-omnistock-mvp`

**Created**: 2026-10-30

**Status**: Draft

**Input**: User description: "Specify the complete OmniStock MVP as a cohesive multi-tenant SaaS Inventory & Order Management System, not just an isolated feature."

## Product Overview
OmniStock is a centralized multi-tenant SaaS platform serving as the single source of truth for inventory and order management across multiple products, warehouses, and sales channels.

### Goals
- Centralize inventory and order operations.
- Ensure 100% inventory accuracy and traceability via an immutable ledger.
- Provide strict multi-tenant isolation and explicit permission-based RBAC.
- Automate warehouse allocation, low-stock alerting, operational dashboard reporting, and resilient channel synchronization.

### Scope (MVP)
- Tenant, User, and Explicit Permission-Based RBAC Management (`PermissionsGuard`).
- Product, Category, Variant, Warehouse, Stock Transfer, Low-Stock Alerting, and Inventory Management (with immutable movement ledger).
- Order Management with explicit state machine and automatic split warehouse allocation.
- Centralized inventory synchronization foundation with HMAC-SHA256 webhook authentication (`SyncWebhookCredential`), idempotency, three-phase transactional retry scheduling, lease-based crash recovery, failure classification, and append-only attempt history (`SyncLog` & `SyncAttempt`).
- Customer management, operational dashboard/reporting, and foundational security/activity audit logging.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Multi-Tenant Onboarding, Explicit RBAC & Isolation (Priority: P1)
As a new merchant (`Tenant Admin`), I want to create an account, manage my users, and enforce explicit permission-based access control so that my data is strictly isolated from other tenants and each operator can perform only authorized actions within my tenant.

**Why this priority**: Foundational requirement for SaaS; data security, tenant isolation, and least-privilege access are non-negotiable (Constitution Principles II & VI).

**Independent Test**: Create two tenants; verify that User A from Tenant 1 cannot view or modify any resource belonging to Tenant 2, verify that a `VIEWER` within Tenant 1 is denied write/mutation permissions (`403 Forbidden`), verify that the last active `ADMIN` of a tenant cannot be demoted or deactivated (`409 LAST_ACTIVE_ADMIN_REQUIRED`), and verify cross-tenant denial and auth events are recorded in `AuditLog`.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:
1. **Given** a unique tenant identifier, **When** a Tenant Admin creates a product, customer, or user, **Then** that resource is only visible to authenticated users with valid active membership in that specific tenant ID.
2. **Given** an authenticated user, **When** they attempt to access a resource belonging to a different tenant ID, **Then** the system denies access (`404`/`403`) and logs a security event in `AuditLog`.
3. **Given** an authenticated user with the `VIEWER` role, **When** they attempt an operation requiring a write or administrative permission (e.g., `inventory:adjust`, `orders:ship`, `users:manage`), **Then** the system denies access by default with `403 Forbidden` (`INSUFFICIENT_PERMISSIONS`).

---

### User Story 2 - Accurate Inventory Tracking, Variants, Stock Transfers & Low-Stock Alerts (Priority: P1)
As a Warehouse Manager, I want every physical stock change (manual adjustments, inter-warehouse transfers, and order shipments) to be recorded in an immutable ledger and low-stock conditions (`available <= low_stock_threshold`) to automatically trigger and resolve in-app alerts per warehouse SKU so that I have a complete audit trail and proactive visibility into stock depletion.

**Why this priority**: Core value proposition (Constitution Principle III); ensures auditability, prevents "magic" stock changes, and surfaces stockout risks before orders fail.

**Independent Test**: Configure a `low_stock_threshold` on a warehouse SKU, perform a stock adjustment and an inter-warehouse stock transfer; verify that `Available` and `In-Transit` quantities update accurately, immutable `StockMovement` records are created, an `OPEN` `LowStockAlert` is created without duplicates when `available <= low_stock_threshold`, and the alert transitions to `RESOLVED` (remaining permanently in historical queries with `ON DELETE RESTRICT` integrity) when `available > low_stock_threshold`.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:
1. **Given** a sellable SKU with 10 units in Warehouse A, **When** a stock adjustment of -2 is made, **Then** `on_hand` and `available` become 8 and an immutable `ADJUSTMENT` `StockMovement` record (`delta: -2`) is created.
2. **Given** an existing `StockMovement` record, **When** any user or process attempts to edit or delete the record, **Then** the database rejects the operation.
3. **Given** 10 available units in Warehouse A and 0 in Warehouse B, **When** a `StockTransfer` of 4 units from A to B is dispatched (`DRAFT -> IN_TRANSIT`), **Then** Warehouse A `on_hand` and `available` decrease to 6 (with a `TRANSFER` `StockMovement` of `-4`), Warehouse B `in_transit` increases to 4 while Warehouse B `available` remains 0, and **When** Warehouse B receives the transfer (`IN_TRANSIT -> RECEIVED`), **Then** Warehouse B `in_transit` becomes 0, `on_hand` and `available` become 4, and a `TRANSFER` `StockMovement` of `+4` is created.
4. **Given** an `InventoryItem` with `low_stock_threshold = 5` and `available = 6`, **When** an order reservation or stock adjustment reduces `available` to `4` (or `0`), **Then** the system creates a single `OPEN` `LowStockAlert` for that `(tenant_id, product_id, warehouse_id)` (updating `current_available` without creating duplicate `OPEN` alerts on further drops), and **When** replenishment raises `available` to `6` (`available > low_stock_threshold`), **Then** the `OPEN` alert automatically transitions to `RESOLVED` with `resolved_at` populated and remains permanently queryable for historical review.

### User Story 3 - Customer Orders, State Machine & Automated Split Allocation (Priority: P1)
As an Order Processor, I want orders to associate with a tenant customer, follow an explicit lifecycle (`DRAFT`, `PENDING`, `ALLOCATED`, `SHIPPED`, `CANCELLED`), and automatically allocate stock across priority-ordered warehouses on an all-or-nothing basis so that we never oversell or leave partial reservations.

**Why this priority**: Operational efficiency and prevention of overselling (Constitution Principle IV).

**Independent Test**: Create a customer and an order for a SKU stored in two priority-ordered warehouses; verify the system creates `OrderAllocation` records across warehouses in priority order, moves quantities from `Available` to `Reserved`, and on shipment decrements `On-Hand` and `Reserved` while creating `SALE` `StockMovement` records.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:
1. **Given** Warehouse A (`priority: 1`, 5 available units) and Warehouse B (`priority: 2`, 10 available units), **When** an order for 12 units is allocated, **Then** the system creates two `OrderAllocation` records for that order item (5 units from Warehouse A and 7 units from Warehouse B), reserves those quantities, and transitions the order to `ALLOCATED`.
2. **Given** total available stock across all active tenant warehouses is 11 units, **When** an order requesting 12 units is submitted for allocation, **Then** the system rejects the allocation with an `INSUFFICIENT_STOCK` error, rolls back the transaction, and leaves zero partial reservations.
3. **Given** an `ALLOCATED` order, **When** the order is shipped (`ALLOCATED -> SHIPPED`), **Then** `on_hand` and `reserved` are atomically decremented in each allocated warehouse and a `SALE` `StockMovement` record is created per allocation.
4. **Given** an `ALLOCATED` order, **When** the order is cancelled (`ALLOCATED -> CANCELLED`), **Then** all reserved quantities are released back to `available`; **Given** a `SHIPPED` order, **When** cancellation is attempted, **Then** the system rejects the invalid state transition.

### User Story 4 - Operational Dashboard, Basic Reporting & Resilient Channel Sync (Priority: P2)
As a Tenant Admin or Warehouse Manager, I want an operational dashboard showing accurate tenant-scoped KPIs and recent activity, and I want external synchronization events to be authenticated via HMAC-SHA256, tracked in `SyncLog` and `SyncAttempt`, automatically retried on transient failures with exponential backoff and lease-based crash recovery, and manually inspectable/acceleratable without duplicating orders or stock movements.

**Why this priority**: Operational visibility and integration reliability (Constitution Principles VIII & X).

**Independent Test**: Query `GET /dashboard/summary` for a tenant with known active/inactive products, customers, warehouses, orders, movements, and open low-stock alerts to verify exact metric calculations (`period_order_value`, active-only inventory/alert summaries, and `[from, to]` filtered recent lists); submit a signed channel webhook that encounters a transient lock/timeout failure, verify `SyncLog` transitions `PENDING -> PROCESSING -> RETRY_SCHEDULED` across isolated transactions while recording a `FAILED_RETRYABLE` `SyncAttempt`, and verify subsequent worker retry or `POST /sync/logs/{id}/retry` acceleration processes the event idempotently without duplicate orders or movements.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:
1. **Given** a tenant with active/inactive products, customers, warehouses, orders across statuses, stock movements, and open low-stock alerts, **When** an authorized user requests `GET /dashboard/summary?from=...&to=...` with ISO-8601 timestamps containing explicit UTC offsets, **Then** the system returns exact tenant-scoped counts, gross committed order value (`period_order_value`: `allocated_order_value`, `shipped_order_value`, `combined_committed_order_value`), active-product/active-warehouse inventory and open alert summaries, and `[from, to]`-filtered `recent_orders` and `recent_stock_movements` without exposing any other tenant's data.
2. **Given** an inbound HMAC-authenticated channel event (`SyncLog`) that enters `PENDING`, is claimed as `PROCESSING` (`attempt_count = 1`), and fails due to a retryable transient database error (e.g., `pg_sqlstate = '55P03'`, `error_code = 'DB_LOCK_TIMEOUT'`), **When** `attempt_count < max_attempts` (`5`), **Then** the system rolls back the domain transaction, records an append-only `SyncAttempt` row in a fresh transaction, and sets `status = 'RETRY_SCHEDULED'` with exponential backoff `next_retry_at`; an authorized user can accelerate a `RETRY_SCHEDULED` event via `POST /sync/logs/{id}/retry` (setting `next_retry_at = CURRENT_TIMESTAMP` for the worker without bypassing `max_attempts = 5`), whereas permanent business failures (`INSUFFICIENT_STOCK`) or exhausted retries (`attempt_count = 5`, `MAX_RETRIES_EXHAUSTED`) transition to terminal `FAILED_PERMANENT`.

### Edge Cases
- **Concurrent Reservations**: Two orders for the last remaining unit are placed at the exact same millisecond. (System MUST use atomic transactions and pessimistic `SELECT FOR UPDATE` locking in deterministic `(product_id ASC, warehouse_id ASC)` order to ensure only one succeeds and the other fails closed with `INSUFFICIENT_STOCK`).
- **Incomplete Transfers**: A stock transfer is dispatched (`IN_TRANSIT`) but the receiving warehouse has not confirmed receipt. (System MUST track `in_transit` quantity separately from `on_hand` and `available`; in-transit units MUST NOT be counted as available stock in either the source or destination warehouse).
- **Invalid Transfer Endpoints**: A user attempts to transfer stock from Warehouse A to Warehouse A, or to a warehouse belonging to another tenant. (System MUST reject same-warehouse and cross-tenant transfers at both validation and database constraint layers).
- **Tenant ID Injection**: A malicious user attempts to pass a different `tenant_id` in an API payload. (System MUST ignore payload `tenant_id` and use the authenticated server-side session context).
- **Repeated Low-Stock Drops**: Available stock for a SKU with `low_stock_threshold = 5` drops from `6 -> 4 -> 2 -> 0`. (System MUST maintain a single `OPEN` `LowStockAlert` for that `(tenant_id, product_id, warehouse_id)`, updating `current_available` on the existing open alert rather than creating duplicate open alerts).
- **Concurrent Sync Retry Workers & Crash Lease Expiry**: Two background retry worker instances poll for due `SyncLog` rows at the same instant, or a worker crashes while a `SyncLog` is in `PROCESSING`. (Workers MUST iterate active tenants from `tenants`, set `SET LOCAL app.current_tenant_id` inside each tenant transaction, claim due `PENDING` / `RETRY_SCHEDULED` rows using `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` with a 60-second `lease_expires_at` and unique `lease_token`, and safely recover expired `PROCESSING` leases (`lease_expires_at <= CURRENT_TIMESTAMP`) by recording the single incomplete attempt as `PROCESSING_LEASE_EXPIRED` without duplicating orders or mutating existing `SyncAttempt` rows).

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

#### 1. Tenant, Security, Explicit RBAC & Customers (FR-TS / FR-CUS)
- **FR-001**: System MUST enforce strict data isolation at the database/storage layer (PostgreSQL RLS) for every tenant-owned entity (all 17 tenant-owned tables: `users`, `audit_logs`, `customers`, `categories`, `products`, `warehouses`, `inventory_items`, `low_stock_alerts`, `stock_movements`, `stock_transfers`, `stock_transfer_items`, `orders`, `order_items`, `order_allocations`, `sync_webhook_credentials`, `sync_logs`, `sync_attempts`).
- **FR-002**: System MUST enforce deny-by-default Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) via `PermissionsGuard` (`@RequirePermissions('<module>:<action>')`) — the sole authoritative RBAC guard (controllers MUST NOT mix coarse `@Roles()` decorators with `@RequirePermissions()`) — with three tenant roles (`ADMIN`, `MANAGER`, `VIEWER`) plus a separate internal non-JWT execution context (`SYSTEM_WORKER`) for background sync retry execution:
  - **Authentication, Database-Backed Role Resolution & Tenant Membership Gate (`F10-2`, `F10-3`)**:
    1. Unauthenticated requests (missing/invalid/expired JWT) MUST be rejected with `401 Unauthorized` (`AUTH_UNAUTHORIZED`) and logged via `record_pre_auth_audit_log`.
    2. Authenticated requests MUST have their `tenant_id` and `user_id` extracted from the verified JWT by `TenantInterceptor`, which sets `SET LOCAL app.current_tenant_id = '<tenant_uuid>'` inside the active transaction and loads the current `User` row from the database (`users`). If `tenant_id` is missing, the `User` row does not exist in the active tenant, or `User.is_active = false`, the request MUST be rejected with `403 Forbidden` (`TENANT_MEMBERSHIP_REQUIRED`) — tenant membership is verified independently of permission checks.
    3. `PermissionsGuard` MUST evaluate the current `User.role` loaded from the database by `TenantInterceptor` (JWT role claims MUST NOT be treated as the authoritative permission source, ensuring immediate enforcement of role changes or deactivations) and reject any request lacking the endpoint's required permission with `403 Forbidden` (`INSUFFICIENT_PERMISSIONS`).
    4. **Last-Admin Self-Lockout Protection (`F10-3`)**: `PATCH /users/{id}` MUST lock active `ADMIN` user rows for the tenant (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`) inside the transaction and reject any attempt to deactivate (`is_active: false`) or demote (`role != 'ADMIN'`) the last active `ADMIN` of a tenant with `409 Conflict` (`LAST_ACTIVE_ADMIN_REQUIRED`).
    5. **Narrow Webhook Route Exemption (`F10-1`)**: `POST /sync/webhook/{source}` is an explicit public-to-JWT webhook route protected by `WebhookSignatureGuard` (HMAC-SHA256 verification against tenant-owned `SyncWebhookCredential`). It is narrowly exempted from `JwtAuthGuard` and `PermissionsGuard`, and MUST NEVER be exempted from `WebhookSignatureGuard`.

#### 2. Catalog, Variants, Inventory & Low-Stock Alerts (FR-INV)
- **FR-004**: System MUST support a flat tenant-scoped `Category` model and `Product` catalog with required tenant-unique `sku` (`NOT NULL`), required `price` (`NOT NULL`, `price >= 0`), optional tenant-unique `barcode`, optional `category_id`, and parent-child product variants (`parent_product_id` + `variant_attributes` JSONB). Stock is tracked per sellable SKU (`Product` with `is_variant_parent = false`); variant parent container products (`is_variant_parent = true`) still have required `sku` and `price` fields but are not sellable and MUST NOT hold stock (`InventoryItem`), movements (`StockMovement`), transfer items (`StockTransferItem`), or order items (`OrderItem`). Both `POST /products` and `PATCH /products/{id}` MUST enforce `price >= 0` (`400 Bad Request` `VALIDATION_ERROR`), tenant-scoped `barcode` uniqueness (`409 Conflict` on duplicate), and parent/variant restrictions.
- **FR-005 (`F2-2`)**: Every physical (`on_hand`) inventory change in the MVP (manual adjustment `ADJUSTMENT`, order shipment `SALE`, stock transfer dispatch/receipt `TRANSFER`) MUST generate an immutable entry in the `StockMovement` ledger (`type` enum: `ADJUSTMENT | SALE | TRANSFER | RETURN`) and be queryable via a paginated tenant-scoped API (`GET /inventory/movements`). In the MVP, `RETURN` is a **reserved enum value** not yet supported by a public return workflow; no endpoint may create a `RETURN` movement in the MVP, and any future return workflow MUST update inventory and evaluate `LowStockAlert` state transactionally.
- **FR-006**: System MUST track four distinct quantity concepts on `InventoryItem`: `on_hand` (physical stock in warehouse), `reserved` (committed to `ALLOCATED` orders), `available` (strictly derived as `on_hand - reserved`, never independently writable), and `in_transit` (inbound stock from `IN_TRANSIT` transfers, never counted in `on_hand` or `available`).
- **FR-007**: System MUST prevent `available`, `on_hand`, `reserved`, and `in_transit` stock from dropping below zero (`on_hand >= 0`, `reserved >= 0`, `reserved <= on_hand`, `in_transit >= 0`).
- **FR-017 (Low-Stock Alerts — `F2`, `F2-1`, `F3-3`)**: System MUST support in-app/API-visible per-warehouse-SKU low-stock alerts (`LowStockAlert`) evaluated transactionally whenever `InventoryItem.available` or `InventoryItem.low_stock_threshold` changes (email, SMS, push notifications, and external notification providers are out of MVP scope):
  - **Threshold Scope & Configuration**: Configured per `(tenant_id, product_id, warehouse_id)` via `InventoryItem.low_stock_threshold` (`INTEGER NULLABLE`, `CHECK (low_stock_threshold IS NULL OR low_stock_threshold >= 0)`). When `low_stock_threshold IS NULL`, low-stock alerting is disabled for that warehouse SKU.
  - **Trigger Condition (`available` vs `on_hand`)**: Evaluated strictly against `available` (`on_hand - reserved`), never `on_hand` or `in_transit`. An `InventoryItem` is in a low-stock state when `low_stock_threshold IS NOT NULL AND available <= low_stock_threshold`. Because `available >= 0` is enforced by database constraints, zero-stock (`available = 0`) triggers an alert whenever `low_stock_threshold >= 0`, and negative stock is impossible (`CHECK (on_hand >= 0)` and `CHECK (reserved <= on_hand)`).
  - **Duplicate Prevention**: At most one `OPEN` `LowStockAlert` may exist per `(tenant_id, product_id, warehouse_id)` at any time, enforced at the PostgreSQL layer via a partial unique index `UNIQUE (tenant_id, inventory_item_id) WHERE status = 'OPEN'`. If an `OPEN` alert already exists and `available` changes while remaining `<= low_stock_threshold`, the existing `OPEN` alert's `current_available`, `threshold`, and `updated_at` are updated in place without creating a second `OPEN` alert.
  - **Automatic Resolution & Permanent Historical Retention (`F2-1`, `F3-3`)**: When `available` increases to `> low_stock_threshold` (via stock adjustment, transfer receipt, or order cancellation) or `low_stock_threshold` is set to `NULL` or `< available`, any existing `OPEN` alert for that `InventoryItem` automatically transitions to `status = 'RESOLVED'` with `resolved_at = CURRENT_TIMESTAMP`. Resolved alerts are retained permanently in `low_stock_alerts` for historical review (`GET /alerts/low-stock?status=RESOLVED`), and the composite foreign key from `low_stock_alerts` to `inventory_items` MUST use `ON DELETE RESTRICT` (matching `Product` and `Warehouse` `ON DELETE RESTRICT` policies) so alert history can never be destroyed by cascading deletion. Deactivating a `Product` or `Warehouse` (`is_active = false`) MUST NOT delete, mutate, or resolve existing `OPEN` or `RESOLVED` alerts.
  - **Audit Trail**: Alert creation (`LOW_STOCK_ALERT_TRIGGERED`), threshold configuration changes (`LOW_STOCK_THRESHOLD_UPDATED`), and alert resolution (`LOW_STOCK_ALERT_RESOLVED`) MUST be recorded in `AuditLog`.

#### 3. Warehouses, Split Allocation & Stock Transfers (FR-WH)
- **FR-008 (`A5-3`)**: System MUST support multiple warehouse locations per tenant, each with a tenant-unique case-insensitive `name` (`409 Conflict` on duplicate in `POST /warehouses` and `PATCH /warehouses/{id}`), an integer `priority` (`priority >= 1`, where lower values indicate higher allocation priority), and `is_active` flag. In the MVP, `PATCH /warehouses/{id}` setting `is_active: false` MUST lock all `InventoryItem` rows for that warehouse (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`) inside a transaction and reject deactivation with `409 Conflict` (`WAREHOUSE_HAS_ACTIVE_STOCK_COMMITMENTS`) if any `InventoryItem` in that warehouse has `reserved > 0` or `in_transit > 0`.
- **FR-009**: System MUST automatically allocate order items across active tenant warehouses by first locking all candidate `InventoryItem` rows in global deterministic `(product_id ASC, warehouse_id ASC)` order (`SELECT FOR UPDATE`), and then evaluating allocation preference in memory using `(Warehouse.priority ASC, Warehouse.created_at ASC, Warehouse.id ASC)` based on `available` stock.
- **FR-010**: System MUST support split-shipment allocation via dedicated `OrderAllocation` records (linking `OrderItem`, `Warehouse`, and `allocated_quantity`) on an **all-or-nothing** basis: if the complete requested quantity for every item in the order cannot be allocated across active warehouses, the transaction MUST roll back and fail with `INSUFFICIENT_STOCK` without leaving partial reservations.
- **FR-016**: System MUST support inter-warehouse `StockTransfer` workflows with explicit states `DRAFT -> IN_TRANSIT -> RECEIVED` and `DRAFT -> CANCELLED`:
  - Same-warehouse (`source_warehouse_id == destination_warehouse_id`) and cross-tenant transfers MUST be rejected.
  - Every state-transition operation (`dispatch`, `receive`, `cancel`) MUST first lock the `StockTransfer` header row (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`) before validating its current `status` inside the same database transaction so concurrent or repeated requests cannot apply stock changes twice (returning `409 Conflict` `INVALID_TRANSFER_TRANSITION` on invalid/repeated state transitions).
  - **Dispatch (`DRAFT -> IN_TRANSIT`)**: Atomically locks the `StockTransfer` header row, locks source and destination `InventoryItem` rows in `(product_id ASC, warehouse_id ASC)` order, verifies source `available >= quantity`, decrements source `on_hand` by `quantity`, creates a source `StockMovement` (`type: TRANSFER`, `delta: -quantity`), increments destination `in_transit` by `quantity`, and evaluates low-stock alert state on the source `InventoryItem`.
  - **Receive (`IN_TRANSIT -> RECEIVED`)**: Partial transfer receipt is NOT supported in MVP; `POST /transfers/{id}/receive` always receives the full dispatched `quantity` for every `StockTransferItem` (receipt can never exceed dispatched quantities). Atomically locks the `StockTransfer` header row, locks destination `InventoryItem` rows in `(product_id ASC, warehouse_id ASC)` order, decrements destination `in_transit` by `quantity`, increments destination `on_hand` by `quantity`, creates a destination `StockMovement` (`type: TRANSFER`, `delta: +quantity`), and evaluates low-stock alert resolution on the destination `InventoryItem`.
  - **Cancel (`DRAFT -> CANCELLED`)**: Allowed only from `DRAFT` after locking the `StockTransfer` header row; transfers in `IN_TRANSIT`, `RECEIVED`, or `CANCELLED` cannot be cancelled.

#### 4. Order Lifecycle & State Machine (FR-ORD)
- **FR-011**: System MUST enforce the authoritative order state machine across five states: `DRAFT`, `PENDING`, `ALLOCATED`, `SHIPPED`, and `CANCELLED`. For all state-transition mutations on an existing order (`submit`, `allocate`, `ship`, `cancel`, `PATCH`), the system MUST lock the `Order` header row using `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` before validating `Order.status` inside the same transaction so concurrent duplicate requests cannot apply inventory changes more than once:

| From State | To State | Trigger / Operation | Required Permission | Inventory & Ledger Side Effects | Invalid / Failure Behavior |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

#### 5. Synchronization, Webhook Auth, Retries & Failure Recovery (FR-SYNC — `F7`, `A9`, `F10-1`)
- **FR-013**: System MUST support HMAC-authenticated, idempotent inbound channel synchronization (`POST /sync/webhook/{source}`) backed by `SyncWebhookCredential` (per-source tenant webhook signing credential), `SyncLog` (logical synchronization event header, lease state, and retry state machine), and `SyncAttempt` (append-only per-attempt execution history):
  - **HMAC-SHA256 Webhook Authentication & Versioned AES-256-GCM Secret Encryption (`F10-1`, `F7-CRYPTO-1`..`F7-CRYPTO-3`)**:
    - `POST /sync/webhook/{source}` is protected by a dedicated `WebhookSignatureGuard` (narrowly exempted from `JwtAuthGuard` and `PermissionsGuard`, never exempted from `WebhookSignatureGuard`). Application bootstrap MUST enable raw-body preservation (`rawBody: true` on `NestFactory.create`) so `WebhookSignatureGuard` verifies the exact original HTTP request bytes (`req.rawBody`) prior to JSON parsing, normalization, reserialization, or body transformation.
    - Requires headers `X-Sync-Tenant-Slug`, `X-Sync-External-Id`, `X-Sync-Timestamp` (Unix epoch seconds validated within a bounded replay window of `±300 seconds`), and `X-Sync-Signature` matching the exact syntax `^v1=[0-9a-f]{64}$`.
    - **Versioned Encryption Keyring & Key Rotation (`F7-CRYPTO-1`)**:
      - Master encryption keys are supplied exclusively via protected environment/KMS configuration (never stored in PostgreSQL): `WEBHOOK_SECRET_ENCRYPTION_CURRENT_KEY_VERSION` (positive integer `>= 1` identifying the key version used for new encryption operations) and `WEBHOOK_SECRET_ENCRYPTION_KEYS` (comma-separated `<version>:<64-lowercase-hex-chars>` mapping, e.g., `1:<64-hex-character-key>,2:<64-hex-character-key>`).
      - At startup, every configured `hexKey` is explicitly validated against `/^[0-9a-f]{64}$/` **before** calling `Buffer.from(hexKey, 'hex')` (preventing Node.js silent hex truncation or uppercase/non-hex acceptance) and verified to decode into a `Buffer` of **exactly 32 bytes** (`byteLength === 32`), with `WEBHOOK_SECRET_ENCRYPTION_CURRENT_KEY_VERSION` guaranteed to exist in the keyring. Any key failing `/^[0-9a-f]{64}$/`, missing current key version, duplicate versions, invalid version numbers (`< 1`), or decoded key length `!== 32` bytes MUST fail application startup closed; the application MUST never silently fall back to another key.
      - Safe key rotation deploys the new key in `WEBHOOK_SECRET_ENCRYPTION_KEYS` while retaining all historical keys still referenced by stored `SyncWebhookCredential` records, updates `WEBHOOK_SECRET_ENCRYPTION_CURRENT_KEY_VERSION` for new encryptions, keeps existing credentials decryptable via their persisted `encryption_key_version`, and supports atomic in-place re-encryption (`SyncCredentialService.reencryptCredentialsToCurrentKeyVersion()`) under the current key version (generating a fresh 12-byte CSPRNG nonce and updating `encrypted_secret`, `encryption_iv`, `encryption_auth_tag`, and `encryption_key_version` without changing `webhook_secret` or `secret_hash`). An old key may be removed from `WEBHOOK_SECRET_ENCRYPTION_KEYS` only after verifying that zero stored credentials (active or retained historical/revoked records, as credential revocation sets `is_active = false` and does not delete rows) reference its version.
    - **Canonical Secret, Ciphertext, AAD & Nonce Contract (`F7-CRYPTO-2`, `F7-CRYPTO-3`)**:
      - On credential creation/rotation (`POST /sync/credentials`), the system generates 32 cryptographically secure random bytes and encodes them as a 64-character lowercase hexadecimal string named `webhook_secret` (`^[0-9a-f]{64}$`), which is the canonical secret representation returned **exactly once** in the `201 Created` response (never returning `signing_key_sha256` or any derived key).
      - The database persists `secret_hash` (`SHA-256(UTF8(webhook_secret))` as 64 lowercase hex chars, `CHECK (secret_hash ~ '^[0-9a-f]{64}$')`, used solely as a fingerprint/lookup value and **never** as an HMAC key), `encrypted_secret` (AES-256-GCM ciphertext of `Buffer.from(webhook_secret, 'utf8')` — the 64 UTF-8 bytes of the 64-character hex string, NOT the 32 decoded random bytes — encoded as **exactly 128 lowercase hexadecimal characters**, `CHECK (encrypted_secret ~ '^[0-9a-f]{128}$')`), `encryption_iv` (fresh 12-byte CSPRNG nonce encoded as 24 lowercase hex chars, `CHECK (encryption_iv ~ '^[0-9a-f]{24}$')`, never reused with the same key and guarded by `UNIQUE (encryption_key_version, encryption_iv)` with automatic fresh-nonce regeneration retry on collision), `encryption_auth_tag` (16-byte GCM authentication tag encoded as 32 lowercase hex chars, `CHECK (encryption_auth_tag ~ '^[0-9a-f]{32}$')`), and `encryption_key_version` (`INTEGER >= 1`).
      - Both AES-256-GCM encryption and decryption MUST bind the exact same Additional Authenticated Data bytes: `AAD = UTF8("${tenant_id}:${lower(source)}:${encryption_key_version}")` (`Buffer.from("${tenant_id}:${source.toLowerCase()}:${encryption_key_version}", 'utf8')`).
    - **Webhook Verification & Safe Constant-Time Comparison (`F7-CRYPTO-2`, `F7-CRYPTO-3`)**:
      - `WebhookSignatureGuard` resolves `tenant_id` from the global `tenants` directory (`WHERE slug = :slug AND is_active = true`), opens a transaction with `SET LOCAL app.current_tenant_id = '<tenant_id>'`, loads the active `SyncWebhookCredential` (`WHERE is_active = true AND revoked_at IS NULL`), selects the 32-byte key from `WEBHOOK_SECRET_ENCRYPTION_KEYS` by `credential.encryption_key_version` (failing closed with `401 WEBHOOK_AUTH_UNAUTHORIZED` and a sanitized operational log if the key version is missing or GCM authentication fails), and decrypts `encrypted_secret` **only in memory** using `AAD = UTF8("${tenant_id}:${lower(source)}:${encryption_key_version}")` to recover the 64-character lowercase hex string `webhook_secret`.
      - Both the external sender and `WebhookSignatureGuard` MUST use `Buffer.from(webhook_secret, 'utf8')` (the 64 UTF-8 bytes of the 64-character hex string) directly as the HMAC-SHA256 key and construct the signed message bytes via `Buffer.concat([Buffer.from(`${timestamp}.`, 'utf8'), req.rawBody])` (`HMAC-SHA256(key = Buffer.from(webhook_secret, 'utf8'), message = Buffer.concat([Buffer.from(`${timestamp}.`, 'utf8'), req.rawBody]))`, preserving the exact original `req.rawBody` `Buffer` without UTF-8 string interpolation). Hex-decoding `webhook_secret` before HMAC, using `secret_hash` as the HMAC key, or applying SHA-256 key derivation is strictly prohibited.
      - `WebhookSignatureGuard` validates `X-Sync-Signature` against `^v1=[0-9a-f]{64}$`, hex-decodes the 64-character digest into a 32-byte `Buffer` (`providedSigBuf`), computes the 32-byte expected HMAC digest `Buffer` (`expectedSigBuf`), verifies `providedSigBuf.byteLength === expectedSigBuf.byteLength` (`32` bytes), and calls `crypto.timingSafeEqual(providedSigBuf, expectedSigBuf)` **only after** the equal-length check passes so malformed inputs never throw an unhandled `RangeError`. Any missing, malformed, wrong-length, expired, revoked, or mismatched signature fails closed with `401 Unauthorized` (`WEBHOOK_AUTH_UNAUTHORIZED`).
      - Plaintext `webhook_secret` is never persisted in PostgreSQL, application logs, `AuditLog`, `SyncLog`, `SyncAttempt`, error messages, or subsequent credential-list responses (`GET /sync/credentials` exposes safe metadata only and never exposes `webhook_secret`, `secret_hash`, `encrypted_secret`, `encryption_iv`, `encryption_auth_tag`, encryption keys, or decrypted values).
  - **Idempotency & Duplicate Webhook Behavior across All States (`F7-5`, `A9`)**:
    - Each inbound event is uniquely identified per tenant by `(tenant_id, source, external_id)` (`UNIQUE (tenant_id, source, external_id)` on `sync_logs`, where `source` and `external_id` are `NOT NULL` non-empty strings).
    - If a `SyncLog` already exists for `(tenant_id, source, external_id)`:
      - `status = 'SUCCEEDED'`: Returns `200 OK` with existing `SyncLog` (including `order_id`).
      - `status IN ('PENDING', 'PROCESSING', 'RETRY_SCHEDULED')`: Returns `202 Accepted` with existing `SyncLog` without starting a duplicate execution.
      - `status = 'FAILED_PERMANENT'`: Returns `409 Conflict` with existing `SyncLog` without re-executing.
  - **Three-Phase Transaction Boundaries (`F7-1`)**:
    1. **Phase A — Ingestion & Claim Transactions**:
       - *Ingestion TX*: A new webhook event is inserted into `sync_logs` with `status = 'PENDING'`, `attempt_count = 0`, `max_attempts = 5` and **committed immediately**.
       - *Claim TX*: Claiming a `PENDING` or due `RETRY_SCHEDULED` event (where `attempt_count < max_attempts`) executes in its own transaction with `SET LOCAL app.current_tenant_id`, locks the `SyncLog` row (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` or `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`), increments `attempt_count = attempt_count + 1` (the **only** place `attempt_count` is ever incremented), sets `status = 'PROCESSING'`, `processing_started_at = CURRENT_TIMESTAMP`, `lease_expires_at = CURRENT_TIMESTAMP + INTERVAL '60 seconds'`, `lease_token = gen_random_uuid()`, `active_trigger_source` (`'WEBHOOK'`, `'AUTO_RETRY'`, or `'MANUAL_RETRY'`), `next_retry_at = NULL`, and `last_attempted_at = CURRENT_TIMESTAMP`, and **commits before domain processing begins**.
    2. **Phase B — Domain Transaction**:
       - Opens a fresh transaction with `SET LOCAL app.current_tenant_id`, locks `SyncLog` (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`), and verifies `status = 'PROCESSING'`, `lease_token = claimed_lease_token`, and `lease_expires_at > CURRENT_TIMESTAMP`.
       - Executes order creation and allocation (`OrderService.create` with `auto_allocate: true`) atomically.
       - **On Success**: Inserts the append-only `SUCCEEDED` `SyncAttempt` (`attempt_number = SyncLog.attempt_count`, `lease_token = claimed_lease_token`) and updates `SyncLog` to `status = 'SUCCEEDED'` (`order_id = order.id`, `completed_at = CURRENT_TIMESTAMP`, clearing lease and error fields) in the **same** transaction as the domain changes, then commits.
       - **On Failure**: Rolls back the complete Phase B domain transaction (`ROLLBACK`), leaving zero partial orders, allocations, or stock movements.
    3. **Phase C — Failure Persistence Transaction**:
       - After Phase B has fully rolled back, opens a **fresh transaction** (never reusing an aborted PostgreSQL transaction), re-establishes tenant context (`SET LOCAL app.current_tenant_id`), locks `SyncLog` (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`) verifying `status = 'PROCESSING'` and `lease_token = claimed_lease_token`, inserts the failed `SyncAttempt` (`attempt_number = SyncLog.attempt_count`, `lease_token = claimed_lease_token`), and atomically updates `SyncLog` status (`RETRY_SCHEDULED` or `FAILED_PERMANENT`), error fields, and `next_retry_at`, clearing lease fields.
  - **Canonical `attempt_count` & Exponential Backoff Rule (`F7-2`)**:
    - `SyncLog.attempt_count` starts at `0` and increments by `1` **exclusively in Phase A** when an execution attempt is claimed.
    - `SyncAttempt.attempt_number` equals the resulting `SyncLog.attempt_count` (`UNIQUE (tenant_id, sync_log_id, attempt_number)`).
    - `max_attempts = 5` across all trigger sources (`WEBHOOK`, `AUTO_RETRY`, `MANUAL_RETRY`); no execution may start when `attempt_count >= max_attempts`.
    - Backoff after failed attempt number $k = \text{SyncLog.attempt\_count}$: $\text{delay\_seconds} = \min(30 \times 2^{k - 1}, 3600)$ (`30s`, `60s`, `120s`, `240s` for $k = 1, 2, 3, 4$). Next retry is scheduled (`status = 'RETRY_SCHEDULED'`, `next_retry_at = CURRENT_TIMESTAMP + delay_seconds`) only if `attempt_status = 'FAILED_RETRYABLE'` and $k < \text{max\_attempts}$. When attempt $k = 5$ fails, `SyncLog` transitions to `status = 'FAILED_PERMANENT'` with `last_error_code = 'MAX_RETRIES_EXHAUSTED'` and `next_retry_at = NULL`.
  - **Distinct Error Namespaces (`F7-4`)**:
    - `SyncAttempt` (`pg_sqlstate`, `http_status`, `error_code`, `error_message`) and `SyncLog` (`last_pg_sqlstate`, `last_http_status`, `last_error_code`, `last_error_message`) store error metadata in strictly separate columns:
      - `pg_sqlstate`: 5-character PostgreSQL SQLSTATE only (e.g., `'40P01'`, `'55P03'`, `'57014'`, `'40001'`).
      - `http_status`: External HTTP response status integer (`100..599`) only, when applicable.
      - `error_code`: Canonical application error identifier only:
        - *Retryable (`FAILED_RETRYABLE`)*: `'DB_DEADLOCK'` (`pg_sqlstate = '40P01'`), `'DB_LOCK_TIMEOUT'` (`pg_sqlstate = '55P03'`), `'DB_QUERY_TIMEOUT'` (`pg_sqlstate = '57014'`), `'DB_SERIALIZATION_FAILURE'` (`pg_sqlstate = '40001'`), `'DB_TRANSIENT_UNAVAILABLE'`, `'PROCESSING_LEASE_EXPIRED'`.
        - *Permanent (`FAILED_PERMANENT`)*: `'INSUFFICIENT_STOCK'`, `'VALIDATION_ERROR'`, `'RESOURCE_NOT_FOUND'`, `'VARIANT_PARENT_NOT_SELLABLE'`, `'MAX_RETRIES_EXHAUSTED'`.
      - `error_message`: Sanitized diagnostic text (MUST NEVER contain credentials, secrets, authorization headers, or sensitive payload data).
  - **Processing Lease Expiry & Crash Recovery (`F7-5`)**:
    - Every `PROCESSING` claim sets a 60-second lease (`lease_expires_at = processing_started_at + INTERVAL '60 seconds'`, `lease_token = UUID`).
    - If a worker/process crashes during Phase B or before Phase C commits, the uncommitted Phase B domain transaction rolls back automatically in PostgreSQL. Once `lease_expires_at <= CURRENT_TIMESTAMP`, `SyncRetryWorker` locks the expired `PROCESSING` row via `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, accounts for the incomplete attempt $k = \text{attempt\_count}$ **exactly once** (without incrementing `attempt_count` and without updating or deleting any existing `SyncAttempt` row) by inserting a `FAILED_RETRYABLE` `SyncAttempt` (`attempt_number = k`, `lease_token = SyncLog.lease_token`, `trigger_source = SyncLog.active_trigger_source`, `error_code = 'PROCESSING_LEASE_EXPIRED'`), clears the lease fields, and transitions `SyncLog` to `RETRY_SCHEDULED` (if $k < 5$) or `FAILED_PERMANENT` with `'MAX_RETRIES_EXHAUSTED'` (if $k = 5$).
  - **Manual Retry Rules (`F7-3`)**:
    - Only `RETRY_SCHEDULED` events (with `attempt_count < max_attempts`) may be manually accelerated via `POST /sync/logs/{id}/retry` (`sync:retry`).
    - Locks the `SyncLog` row (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`) before validating `status`, rejects `PENDING`, `PROCESSING`, `SUCCEEDED`, and `FAILED_PERMANENT` with `409 Conflict` (`INVALID_SYNC_RETRY_STATE`), and sets `next_retry_at = CURRENT_TIMESTAMP` and `next_trigger_source = 'MANUAL_RETRY'` **without executing domain logic inside the HTTP request**, without incrementing or resetting `attempt_count`, and without increasing `max_attempts`.
    - `SyncRetryWorker` performs the next actual execution attempt (`trigger_source = 'MANUAL_RETRY'`) and increments `attempt_count` in Phase A when claiming it. Permanent business failures (`FAILED_PERMANENT`) and retry-exhausted logs (`MAX_RETRIES_EXHAUSTED`) remain terminal in the MVP and cannot be manually retried.
  - **Worker Algorithm & RLS Compatibility (`F7-6`)**:
    - `SyncRetryWorker` runs under the internal `SYSTEM_WORKER` execution identity using the standard `omnistock_app` database role (`NOBYPASSRLS`, `NOSUPERUSER`) and **never queries `sync_logs` globally before tenant context is set**:
      1. Reads active tenant IDs from the global non-RLS `tenants` directory (`SELECT id FROM tenants WHERE is_active = true ORDER BY id ASC`).
      2. For each `tenant_id`, opens a database transaction and executes `SET LOCAL app.current_tenant_id = '<tenant_id>'`.
      3. Recovers any expired `PROCESSING` leases (`status = 'PROCESSING' AND lease_expires_at <= CURRENT_TIMESTAMP`) using `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`.
      4. Selects due `SyncLog` rows (`(status = 'PENDING' OR (status = 'RETRY_SCHEDULED' AND next_retry_at <= CURRENT_TIMESTAMP)) AND attempt_count < max_attempts`) for that tenant using `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, claims them (Phase A: incrementing `attempt_count`, setting `status = 'PROCESSING'` and lease metadata), and commits the claim transaction.
      5. Executes each claimed event in its own Phase B domain transaction (with `SET LOCAL app.current_tenant_id`) and, on failure, persists the failure in a fresh Phase C transaction (with `SET LOCAL app.current_tenant_id`).

#### 6. Operational Dashboard & Basic Reporting (FR-REP — `F3`, `F3-1`, `F3-2`, `F3-3`)
- **FR-018**: System MUST provide a tenant-scoped operational dashboard endpoint (`GET /dashboard/summary`, requiring `dashboard:read`) with exact, deterministic metric definitions, explicit UTC ISO-8601 date-range filtering (`from`, `to`), optional `warehouse_id` filter, and bounded recent-activity lists (`limit` default `10`, max `50`):
  - **Date-Range Input & Boundary Rules (`F3-2`)**:
    - When supplied, `from` and `to` MUST be full ISO-8601 timestamps containing an explicit UTC offset (`Z` or `±HH:MM`, e.g., `2026-10-01T00:00:00Z` or `2026-10-01T03:00:00+03:00`). Non-UTC offsets are accepted and normalized to UTC. When omitted, `to` defaults to current UTC time (`now()`) and `from` defaults to `to - 30 days` in UTC.
    - Date-only strings (e.g., `2026-10-01`), timestamps without an explicit UTC offset, or ranges where `from > to` MUST be rejected with `400 Bad Request` (`VALIDATION_ERROR`).
    - Inclusive UTC boundaries (`created_at >= from AND created_at <= to`) apply to `orders_by_status`, `period_order_value`, `recent_orders`, and `recent_stock_movements`.
  - **Active-Entity & Metric Definitions (`F3-1`, `F3-3`)**:
    - **`total_products`**: Count of active sellable SKUs (`products` where `tenant_id = current_tenant AND is_variant_parent = false AND is_active = true`).
    - **`total_variant_parents`**: Separate count of active variant parent containers (`products` where `tenant_id = current_tenant AND is_variant_parent = true AND is_active = true`).
    - **`total_customers`**: Count of `customers` belonging to the active tenant.
    - **`total_warehouses`**: Count of active `warehouses` (`is_active = true`) belonging to the active tenant.
    - **`orders_by_status`**: Object mapping each of the five order statuses (`DRAFT`, `PENDING`, `ALLOCATED`, `SHIPPED`, `CANCELLED`) to the count of tenant `orders` with `created_at` within `[from, to]`.
    - **`period_order_value` (`F3-1`)**: Gross order value (`SUM(Order.total_amount)`) for tenant orders with `created_at` within `[from, to]`, split explicitly into `allocated_order_value` (`status = 'ALLOCATED'`), `shipped_order_value` (`status = 'SHIPPED'`), and `combined_committed_order_value` (`status IN ('ALLOCATED', 'SHIPPED')`). `DRAFT`, `PENDING`, and `CANCELLED` orders are excluded. These metrics are explicitly defined as **gross order values (`SUM(Order.total_amount)`)**, never collected payments, recognized accounting revenue, profit, or COGS.
    - **`inventory_quantity_summary` & Explicit Exclusion of Inventory Cost Valuation (`F3-3`)**: Aggregate `total_on_hand`, `total_reserved`, `total_available` (`total_on_hand - total_reserved`), and `total_in_transit` across tenant `inventory_items` belonging to **active products (`Product.is_active = true`) in active warehouses (`Warehouse.is_active = true`) only** (further filtered by `warehouse_id` when provided). Because `Product` in the MVP defines only selling `price` and does **not** model unit cost/COGS (`cost_price`), **inventory cost/valuation is explicitly excluded from the MVP**.
    - **`low_stock_alerts_count` (`F3-3`)**: Count of currently `OPEN` `LowStockAlert` records (`status = 'OPEN'`) belonging to **active products (`Product.is_active = true`) in active warehouses (`Warehouse.is_active = true`) only** (further filtered by `warehouse_id` when provided).
    - **`recent_orders` (`F3-2`)**: Top $N$ (`limit`, default `10`, max `50`) tenant `orders` with `created_at` within the normalized `[from, to]` range, ordered deterministically by `created_at DESC, id DESC`.
    - **`recent_stock_movements` (`F3-2`)**: Top $N$ (`limit`, default `10`, max `50`) tenant `stock_movements` with `created_at` within the normalized `[from, to]` range (and filtered by `warehouse_id` when provided), ordered deterministically by `created_at DESC, id DESC`.

#### 7. Operational Readiness, API Quality, Observability & Lifecycle Requirements (FR-OPS — Batch 4)
- **FR-019 (Environment Separation, JWT Secret Material Validation & Startup Effective Database Privilege Verification)**:
  - **Runtime vs. Migration Environment Separation**: The runtime application process MUST load only runtime database variables (`DB_HOST`, `DB_PORT`, `DB_NAME`, `DB_APP_USER`, `DB_APP_PASSWORD`, `DB_POOL_MAX`). Migration credentials (`DB_MIGRATOR_USER`, `DB_MIGRATOR_PASSWORD`) are restricted exclusively to the TypeORM migration CLI (`src/common/database/data-source.ts`) and isolated test database provisioning, and MUST NEVER be loaded into the runtime application configuration service.
  - **JWT Secret Length vs. Cryptographic Entropy Validation**: `JWT_SECRET` MUST contain at least **32 UTF-8 bytes (`>= 32` bytes)** validated deterministically via `Buffer.byteLength(JWT_SECRET, 'utf8') >= 32` (after rejecting empty or whitespace-only values). Explicitly: `Buffer.byteLength(JWT_SECRET, 'utf8') >= 32` is a **length requirement only** and does **not** prove sufficient cryptographic entropy, and a non-repeating-character check is a basic sanity check only that MUST NOT be treated or described as an entropy guarantee. Production `JWT_SECRET` values (`NODE_ENV === 'production'`) MUST be generated using a cryptographically secure random number generator (CSPRNG) with at least 32 random bytes (e.g., `crypto.randomBytes(32).toString('hex')` or base64). Startup MUST fail closed in all environments if `Buffer.byteLength(JWT_SECRET, 'utf8') < 32`, if `JWT_SECRET` is empty/whitespace-only, or if `JWT_SECRET` consists of a repeated single character, and in `production` (`NODE_ENV === 'production'`) startup MUST additionally fail closed on any missing, weak, known-placeholder, or otherwise invalid secret (e.g., `changeme`, `secret`, `jwt_secret`, `default`, `development`, `test`, `password`, `omnistock`, `replace_me`, `your-256-bit-secret`, or the `.env.example` placeholder). Development and test environments (`NODE_ENV === 'development' | 'test'`) may use environment-specific secrets subject to their documented validation rules (`Buffer.byteLength(JWT_SECRET, 'utf8') >= 32`, non-empty/non-whitespace, and not a repeated single character).
  - **Startup Effective PostgreSQL Privilege & Role-Membership Verification**: A username string inequality check alone is insufficient. During `DatabaseModule` startup initialization (`onModuleInit`), the runtime application MUST query PostgreSQL system catalogs (`pg_roles`, `pg_auth_members`, `pg_class`, `pg_namespace`) and built-in privilege-evaluation functions (`pg_has_role()`, `has_table_privilege()`, `has_schema_privilege()`) over the active runtime connection and fail startup closed if any of the following checks fail:
    1. **Role Attributes & Inherited Memberships**: Neither `current_user` nor any role of which `current_user` is a direct or transitive member (`pg_has_role(current_user, r.oid, 'MEMBER')` or `'USAGE'`) may have `rolsuper = true`, `rolbypassrls = true`, `rolcreatedb = true`, or `rolcreaterole = true`.
    2. **Prohibited Role Membership**: `pg_has_role(current_user, 'omnistock_migrator', 'MEMBER')`, `pg_has_role(current_user, 'omnistock_preauth_auditor', 'MEMBER')`, `pg_has_role(current_user, 'pg_read_all_data', 'MEMBER')`, and `pg_has_role(current_user, 'pg_write_all_data', 'MEMBER')` MUST all evaluate to `false`.
    3. **Schema & Table Non-Ownership**: Neither `current_user` nor any role granted to `current_user` (`pg_has_role(current_user, owner_oid, 'MEMBER')`) may own the `public` schema (`pg_namespace.nspowner`) or any of the 18 application tables (`tenants` + all 17 tenant-owned tables in `pg_class.relowner`), and `has_schema_privilege(current_user, 'public', 'CREATE')` MUST be `false`.
    4. **Effective Append-Only & Table Privileges**: Using `has_table_privilege(current_user, 'public.<table>', '<privilege>')`, `current_user` MUST have `UPDATE = false`, `DELETE = false`, `TRUNCATE = false`, `TRIGGER = false`, and `REFERENCES = false` on all 3 append-only tables (`stock_movements`, `audit_logs`, `sync_attempts`), and `TRUNCATE = false` and `TRIGGER = false` on all 18 application tables.
    5. **RLS Active Check**: All 17 tenant-owned tables MUST have `relrowsecurity = true` (`ENABLE ROW LEVEL SECURITY`) and `relforcerowsecurity = true` (`FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY`) in `pg_class`.
- **FR-020 (API Conventions, Pagination, Request Validation & Two-Tier Webhook Rate Limiting)**:
  - **Canonical Error Response Envelope**: All `4xx` and `5xx` HTTP responses MUST be formatted by `ApiExceptionFilter` as `{ "statusCode": number, "error_code": string, "message": string, "details"?: object, "request_id": string, "timestamp": string }`, suppressing internal stack traces and raw SQL diagnostics on unhandled `500 INTERNAL_SERVER_ERROR` responses.
  - **Standard Pagination Envelope**: All 10 paginated `GET` list endpoints (`GET /users`, `GET /audit-logs`, `GET /customers`, `GET /products`, `GET /inventory/levels`, `GET /inventory/movements`, `GET /alerts/low-stock`, `GET /transfers`, `GET /orders`, `GET /sync/logs`) MUST validate `page` (default `1`, integer `>= 1`) and `limit` (default `20`, integer `1..100`; returning `400 Bad Request` `VALIDATION_ERROR` otherwise) and return `{ "data": T[], "page": number, "limit": number, "total": number }`.
  - **Strict Request Validation**: Global `ValidationPipe` MUST enforce `whitelist: true`, `forbidNonWhitelisted: true`, and `transform: true`, rejecting unknown properties (including any client-supplied `tenant_id` in request bodies) with `400 Bad Request` (`VALIDATION_ERROR`).
  - **Trusted Client IP Resolution & Public / Two-Tier Webhook Rate Limiting (`429 Too Many Requests` / `RATE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`)**:
    - **Trusted Client IP (`client_ip`) Resolution Policy**: Rate limiting MUST NEVER trust arbitrary client-supplied `X-Forwarded-For` headers. Forwarding headers (`X-Forwarded-For`) may ONLY be honored when the immediate connecting socket peer (`req.socket.remoteAddress`) matches an explicitly configured trusted proxy in `TRUSTED_PROXY_IPS` (comma-separated IP/CIDR list, defaulting to empty/disabled so no proxy is trusted by default). When the immediate connecting peer is **not** a configured trusted proxy, the application MUST ignore `X-Forwarded-For` completely and use the direct socket address (`req.socket.remoteAddress`) as `client_ip`. When the immediate connecting peer **is** an explicitly configured trusted proxy, `client_ip` MUST be resolved using a right-to-left trusted-proxy chain policy — inspecting `X-Forwarded-For` hops from rightmost to leftmost and selecting the first valid IP address that is not in `TRUSTED_PROXY_IPS` (falling back to `req.socket.remoteAddress` if absent or malformed) — so clients cannot bypass rate limits by prepending or injecting fake `X-Forwarded-For` entries. This exact `client_ip` resolution policy applies consistently to all IP-based rate limits below (`POST /tenants`, `POST /auth/login`, and pre-authentication webhook rate limits on `POST /sync/webhook/{source}`).
    - **Rate-Limit Enforcement (`RATE_LIMIT_TTL_SECONDS`, default `60`s)**: Rate-limited endpoints MUST return `429 Too Many Requests` (`error_code: "RATE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED"`) with a `Retry-After` response header (in integer seconds):
      1. `POST /tenants`: Keyed by resolved `client_ip` (`tenant_onboard:ip:${client_ip}`, default `RATE_LIMIT_TENANT_ONBOARD_MAX = 5` per window).
      2. `POST /auth/login`: Keyed by composite account and resolved `client_ip` key (`login:account:${client_ip}:${lower(tenant_slug)}:${lower(email)}`, default `RATE_LIMIT_LOGIN_MAX = 10` per window) paired with a coarse per-IP ceiling (`login:ip:${client_ip}`, default `RATE_LIMIT_LOGIN_IP_MAX = 30` per window).
      3. `POST /sync/webhook/{source}` (**Two-Tier Pre-Auth & Post-HMAC Rate Limiting**):
         - **Pre-Authentication Tier (Before `WebhookSignatureGuard` HMAC Verification)**: `X-Sync-Tenant-Slug` MUST NEVER be trusted as an authenticated tenant identity or rate-limit key before HMAC verification (preventing unauthenticated attackers from spoofing `X-Sync-Tenant-Slug` to exhaust a target tenant's webhook quota). Pre-authentication rate limiting enforces a per-IP limit (`webhook:preauth:ip:${client_ip}`, default `RATE_LIMIT_WEBHOOK_IP_MAX = 60` per window) and a per-source-and-IP limit (`webhook:preauth:source_ip:${lower(source)}:${client_ip}`, default `RATE_LIMIT_WEBHOOK_SOURCE_IP_MAX = 120` per window) using the trusted `client_ip` resolution policy above.
         - **Post-HMAC Verification Tier (Authenticated Tenant Bucket)**: Only **after** `WebhookSignatureGuard` has resolved the active `Tenant`, decrypted `SyncWebhookCredential`, and verified the `HMAC-SHA256` signature via `crypto.timingSafeEqual`, the system enforces the authenticated tenant-and-source rate limit keyed strictly by the verified `tenant_id` and normalized `source` (`webhook:tenant:${verified_tenant_id}:${lower(source)}`, default `RATE_LIMIT_WEBHOOK_TENANT_MAX = 120` per window).
- **FR-021 (Observability, Correlation IDs, Trusted Log Context & Sensitive-Data Redaction)**:
  - **Correlation IDs (`X-Request-Id`)**: Every HTTP request MUST accept an optional `X-Request-Id` header if it matches `^[A-Za-z0-9._-]{1,64}$` (or generate a fresh UUIDv4 if absent/invalid), echo `X-Request-Id` on the HTTP response, and include `request_id` in structured JSON logs and error envelopes.
  - **Trusted Log Context Only**: Structured JSON log entries MUST populate `tenant_id` and `user_id` **exclusively** from authenticated and database-validated server-side request context (`TenantInterceptor`, `WebhookSignatureGuard` after HMAC verification, or `SyncRetryWorker` active tenant context) and MUST NEVER populate `tenant_id` or `user_id` from unverified client headers, query parameters, or request bodies.
  - **Sensitive-Data Redaction**: Application logs MUST redact and never output `password`, `admin_password`, `password_hash`, JWTs, `Authorization` headers, `webhook_secret`, `encrypted_secret`, `secret_hash`, `WEBHOOK_SECRET_ENCRYPTION_KEYS`, or `X-Sync-Signature`.
- **FR-022 (Health/Readiness Probes, In-Flight Request/Transaction Tracking & Graceful Shutdown)**:
  - **Health & Readiness Probes (`GET /health/live`, `GET /health/ready`)**: Narrowly exempted from `JwtAuthGuard` and `PermissionsGuard` (bringing the total exempt endpoints to five alongside `POST /tenants`, `POST /auth/login`, and `POST /sync/webhook/{source}`):
    - `GET /health/live`: Returns `200 OK` (`{ "status": "ok" }`) with zero database or external I/O dependency.
    - `GET /health/ready`: Executes `SELECT 1` over the runtime `omnistock_app` pool without querying any tenant-owned table or setting `app.current_tenant_id`; returns `200 OK` (`{ "status": "ready" }`) when healthy and not shutting down, or `503 Service Unavailable` (`{ "status": "not_ready" }`) during graceful shutdown or database unavailability, without exposing internal infrastructure details.
  - **In-Flight Request & Complete Multi-Phase Worker Job Tracking on Graceful Shutdown (`SIGTERM` / `SIGINT`)**:
    - The application MUST maintain an explicit in-flight HTTP request tracker (`ShutdownDrainInterceptor` tracking active HTTP request promises) and a **complete multi-phase `SyncLog` job tracker** (tracking each `SyncLog` processing job as a **single continuous logical unit** from initial start until either (a) `Phase B` commits `SUCCEEDED` or (b) `Phase C` failure handling commits `RETRY_SCHEDULED` or `FAILED_PERMANENT`).
    - The in-flight `SyncLog` job tracker MUST remain active continuously across all relevant phases of a job — `Phase A.1 — Ingestion Transaction` (when applicable for inbound webhooks), `Phase A.2 — Claim Transaction`, `Phase B — Domain Transaction`, and `Phase C — Failure Transaction` — and MUST NEVER be released at intermediate transaction boundaries between `Phase A`, `Phase B`, and `Phase C` (releasing only in an outer `finally` block after the complete multi-phase job reaches its terminal `Phase B` or `Phase C` outcome).
    - Upon receiving `SIGTERM` or `SIGINT` (`app.enableShutdownHooks()`):
      1. Immediately transition readiness to `not_ready` (`GET /health/ready` returns `503`) and stop accepting new HTTP connections.
      2. Immediately clear the `SyncRetryWorker` polling timer and set `isShuttingDown = true` so no new worker jobs, tenant iterations, or `Phase A` `SyncLog` claims are accepted.
      3. In `beforeApplicationShutdown`, await completion of all currently tracked in-flight HTTP requests and all currently tracked multi-phase `SyncLog` processing jobs across their full lifecycle (explicitly waiting for `Phase C` failure persistence to finish whenever `Phase B` fails) up to `SHUTDOWN_DRAIN_TIMEOUT_MS` (default `15000`ms), **preventing TypeORM `DataSource` pool shutdown while tracked requests or multi-phase jobs are still executing**.
      4. If `SHUTDOWN_DRAIN_TIMEOUT_MS` elapses before all tracked jobs finish, log a structured shutdown-drain timeout warning with the unfinished job count **without silently treating or marking unfinished jobs as completed** (leaving any interrupted `PROCESSING` `SyncLog` row to be recovered deterministically via its 60-second `lease_expires_at` lease), and close the TypeORM `DataSource` connection pool in `onApplicationShutdown` only after the drain procedure finishes.
- **FR-023 (Database Test Isolation, Backup/Restore Role Bootstrap & Keyring Retention)**:
  - **Database Test Isolation**: Automated unit, integration, and E2E assertions MUST execute using `omnistock_app` (`NOBYPASSRLS`). `omnistock_migrator` MUST only be used for migrations and isolated test-database provisioning/reset (restricted strictly to verified `*_test` database names with `NODE_ENV === 'test'`), never for application/request assertions, and `omnistock_app` MUST NEVER be granted extra privileges for test cleanup. Automatic `SyncRetryWorker` timer polling is disabled during HTTP integration tests (`SYNC_WORKER_ENABLED=false`) in favor of deterministic manual worker ticks (`runTick()`).
  - **Backup, Role Bootstrap & Isolated Restore Verification**: Because `pg_dump --format=custom --no-owner --no-acl` excludes cluster roles, table ownership, and role ACLs, restore procedures MUST:
    1. Bootstrap cluster roles (`omnistock_migrator`, `omnistock_app`, `omnistock_preauth_auditor`) via version-controlled SQL before restoration;
    2. Restore into an isolated target database as `omnistock_migrator` and re-apply/verify role ownership and least-privilege grants (`T039`) before starting application instances;
    3. Retain all historical and current `WEBHOOK_SECRET_ENCRYPTION_KEYS` versions required by `SELECT DISTINCT encryption_key_version FROM sync_webhook_credentials` in external secret management without ever embedding encryption keys in database backups; and
    4. Verify RLS (`ENABLE` + `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` and policies on all 17 tenant-owned tables), append-only triggers (`stock_movements`, `audit_logs`, `sync_attempts`), effective `omnistock_app` privileges (`has_table_privilege()`, `pg_has_role()`), and keyring compatibility before routing traffic.

### Key Entities
- **Tenant**: Global root organizational unit (`id`, `name`, `slug`, `is_active`, `created_at`).
- **User**: Tenant-scoped operator (`id`, `tenant_id`, `email`, `role`: `ADMIN | MANAGER | VIEWER`, `is_active`).
- **AuditLog**: Immutable security and business activity log (`id`, `tenant_id`, `actor_user_id`, `action`, `entity_type`, `entity_id`, `metadata`, `created_at`).
- **Customer**: Tenant-scoped buyer (`id`, `tenant_id`, `name`, `email`, `phone`, `created_at`, `updated_at`).
- **Category**: Flat tenant-scoped product classification (`id`, `tenant_id`, `name`, `description`, `created_at`).
- **Product (SKU / Variant)**: Catalog item (`id`, `tenant_id`, `category_id`, `parent_product_id`, `is_variant_parent`, `sku`, `barcode`, `name`, `description`, `variant_attributes`, `price`, `is_active`).
- **Warehouse**: Physical stock location (`id`, `tenant_id`, `name`, `location`, `priority`, `is_active`).
- **InventoryItem**: Stock state per `(tenant_id, product_id, warehouse_id)` (`on_hand`, `reserved`, derived `available`, `in_transit`, `low_stock_threshold`).
- **LowStockAlert**: Per-warehouse-SKU low-stock alert record (`id`, `tenant_id`, `inventory_item_id` with `ON DELETE RESTRICT`, `product_id`, `warehouse_id`, `threshold`, `current_available`, `status`: `OPEN | RESOLVED`, `triggered_at`, `resolved_at`, `updated_at`).
- **StockMovement**: Immutable physical inventory ledger (`id`, `tenant_id`, `product_id`, `warehouse_id`, `delta`, `type`: `ADJUSTMENT | SALE | TRANSFER | RETURN` where `RETURN` is reserved in MVP, `reference_id`, `created_at`).
- **StockTransfer & StockTransferItem**: Inter-warehouse transfer header (`source_warehouse_id`, `destination_warehouse_id`, `status`: `DRAFT | IN_TRANSIT | RECEIVED | CANCELLED`) and line items (`product_id`, `quantity`).
- **Order & OrderItem**: Customer purchase request (`customer_id`, `customer_name_snapshot`, `customer_email_snapshot`, `status`: `DRAFT | PENDING | ALLOCATED | SHIPPED | CANCELLED`, `total_amount`) and requested SKU line items (`product_id`, `quantity`, `unit_price`).
- **OrderAllocation**: Per-warehouse reservation/fulfillment split for an `OrderItem` (`order_id`, `order_item_id`, `product_id`, `warehouse_id`, `allocated_quantity`).
- **SyncWebhookCredential**: Tenant-owned per-source HMAC-SHA256 webhook signing credential (`id`, `tenant_id`, `source`, `secret_hash`, `encrypted_secret`, `encryption_iv`, `encryption_auth_tag`, `encryption_key_version`, `is_active`, `created_at`, `revoked_at`).
- **SyncLog & SyncAttempt**: External channel webhook idempotency, lease, and retry state record (`id`, `tenant_id`, `source`, `external_id`, `event_type`, `status`: `PENDING | PROCESSING | SUCCEEDED | RETRY_SCHEDULED | FAILED_PERMANENT`, `attempt_count`, `max_attempts`, `next_retry_at`, `next_trigger_source`, `processing_started_at`, `lease_expires_at`, `lease_token`, `active_trigger_source`, `last_pg_sqlstate`, `last_http_status`, `last_error_code`, `last_error_message`, `order_id`, `payload`, `last_attempted_at`, `completed_at`) and append-only attempt execution history (`id`, `tenant_id`, `sync_log_id`, `attempt_number`, `lease_token`, `trigger_source`, `status`, `pg_sqlstate`, `http_status`, `error_code`, `error_message`, `started_at`, `finished_at`, `duration_ms`).

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes
- **SC-001**: **Zero Over-allocation**: Under load testing (100+ concurrent orders for the same SKU), the system never allows total `Reserved` stock to exceed `On-Hand` stock and never leaves partial reservations on failed allocations.
- **SC-002**: **Audit Trail Integrity**: 100% of physical (`on_hand`) inventory quantity changes match the sum of their corresponding `StockMovement` records.
- **SC-003**: **Isolation & RBAC Verification**: Automated security scans confirm that zero cross-tenant data leaks occur in the API, worker, or database layer and 100% of endpoints enforce explicit `PermissionsGuard` or `WebhookSignatureGuard` checks.
- **SC-004**: **Performance**: Automatic warehouse allocation for a 10-item order completes in under 500ms.

## Assumptions
- **Stable Connectivity**: External channel synchronization assumes the channel delivers signed webhooks; OmniStock handles retries and lease recovery for intermittent failures via `SyncLog` and `SyncAttempt`.
- **No Native Billing**: SaaS billing/subscriptions are managed externally or deferred to a later phase (out of scope for MVP).
- **In-App Low-Stock Alerting Only**: Low-stock alerts are exposed via API/dashboard in MVP; external email/SMS/push notifications are out of scope.
- **Web-First**: The MVP is primarily accessed via a web-based dashboard and API; native mobile apps are out of scope.
- **Auth Provider**: Standard JWT-based authentication is used for users; HMAC-SHA256 (`WebhookSignatureGuard`) is used for inbound channel webhooks.

## MVP Boundaries & Out-of-Scope
- **OUT-001**: Native SaaS billing, payment collection, accounting revenue recognition, and subscription lifecycle management.
- **OUT-002**: Advanced AI-driven demand forecasting, BI data warehousing, or inventory cost/COGS accounting valuation (`cost_price` is not modeled in MVP).
- **OUT-003**: Complex logic for shipping label generation, carrier integrations, or public customer return workflows (`StockMovement.type = 'RETURN'` is a reserved enum value in MVP).
- **OUT-004**: Support for non-transactional "negative stock" modes.
- **OUT-005**: External email, SMS, push, or webhook outbound notifications for low-stock alerts.
